On 2026-09-15, after several large multi-part turns (full specs, reviews, many open questions at once), the user said:
*"I would like to go step by step, not the whole thing at a time. I am literally learning via you."* They want to learn
how big game studios run projects, design and architect — and run that process on Phoenix.

**Why:** big batches of specs, findings and decision lists outpaced their learning; they could not absorb or decide.

**How to apply:**
- One topic per turn: explain the concept (how studios do it) → show how it applies to Phoenix → ask at most one or two
  decisions → stop.
- Keep pending decisions parked and name them only when the current step reaches them.
- Prefer short, plain explanations with a concrete Phoenix example over exhaustive tables.
- Still verify facts; mark general industry knowledge that was not checked against a source.
